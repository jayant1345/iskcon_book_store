📦 Shipping & Return Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Shipping
  • We ship physical books to all serviceable pin codes across India via our courier partners.
  • Orders are typically dispatched within 1–3 business days of confirmation.
  • Standard delivery takes 3–7 business days after dispatch, depending on your location.
  • A shipping charge of ₹{{ '%.0f'|format(shipping_charge) if shipping_charge is defined else '50' }} applies per order, and is waived for orders above ₹{{ '%.0f'|format(free_shipping_above) if free_shipping_above is defined else '500' }}.
  • eBooks are delivered instantly via a download link to your registered email/account — no physical shipping is involved.
  • You can track your order status anytime from the Track Order page.
Returns
  • We accept returns of physical books within 7 days of delivery if the item received is damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered.
  • To initiate a return, contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photos of the issue.
  • The book must be unused and in its original condition/packaging to be eligible for return.
  • eBooks and digital downloads, once delivered, are not eligible for return.
