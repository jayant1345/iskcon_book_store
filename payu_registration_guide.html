This document will guide you through the process of registering our temple trust on PayU India — one of India's largest and most trusted payment platforms used by thousands of NGOs, trusts, and religious organisations. Once registered, devotees visiting our online book store can pay via UPI, QR Code, Debit/Credit Card, Net Banking and more. Registration is free and takes about 2 working days to activate.

FieldWhat to Enter

PAN verification is instant — usually done within a few seconds.

6

Upload Documents

Keep the following documents ready as clear scanned copies or photographs (JPG or PDF format, under 5MB each):

A) Trust Entity Documents
  • Registered Trust Deed
    Must be notarized OR verified/stamped by the Registrar office. This is the most important document.
  • List of Trustees
    Typed on temple letterhead — list all trustee names, their roles, and signatures. Must be signed and stamped by an authorised trustee.
  • No Profit Declaration Letter
    A declaration that no individual or entity holds any shareholding or profit in the trust. See sample wording below.
  • Authorization / BR Letter
    Letter on temple letterhead stating the trustee is authorised to sign up for payment gateway services with PayU. Must be signed and dated.

Sample Wording — No Profit Declaration Letter

(Print on temple letterhead, sign and stamp) We, the trustees of ISKCON Ahmedabad, hereby declare that this is a registered religious charitable trust. No individual or entity holds any shareholding or profit interest in this trust. All funds collected are used exclusively for religious and charitable purposes. Authorised Signatory: ___________________ Name: ___________________ Designation: Trustee Date: ___________________ Stamp: [Temple Stamp]

Sample Wording — Authorization Letter

(Print on temple letterhead, sign and stamp) This is to certify that [Trustee Name], Trustee of ISKCON Ahmedabad, is hereby authorised to enter into an agreement for payment gateway services with PayU Payments Private Limited on behalf of the trust. Authorised Signatory: ___________________ Name: ___________________ Designation: Trustee Date: ___________________ Stamp: [Temple Stamp]

B) Trustee / Authorised Person Identity
  • PAN Card of Trustee
    Personal PAN of the trustee who is registering. Clear front-side photograph.
  • Aadhaar Card of Trustee
    Front and back. Or use Voter ID / Passport / Driving License as address proof.
  • Photograph of Trustee
    Recent passport-size photograph (may be requested separately).

C) Bank Account Proof (any ONE)
  • Cancelled Cheque
    Must show trust name printed on it. Write "CANCELLED" across the cheque clearly.
  • Bank Statement (last 3 months)
    First page showing trust name, account number, and IFSC code. Bank stamp preferred.
  • Bank Passbook (first page)
    Clearly showing trust name, account number, branch address, and IFSC.

D) Optional but Very Helpful
  • 12A Certificate
    Income Tax exemption certificate — strongly shows trust is genuine and legitimate.
  • 80G Certificate
    Tax benefit certificate for donors — further proves legitimacy of the trust.
  • Registration Certificate from Charity Commissioner
    If the trust is separately registered under Bombay Public Trust Act or similar.
7

Website Compliance Check

PayU will review our website before giving live access. Make sure our book store has these pages:

  • Home Page
    Clearly shows ISKCON Ahmedabad name and book store purpose
  • Products / Books Page
    Books listed with prices in Indian Rupees (₹)
  • Contact Us Page
    Temple address, email, and phone number
  • Refund / Return Policy
    Simple policy explaining what happens if a devotee wants to return a book
  • Privacy Policy
    Basic statement about how customer data is used
  • Shipping Policy
    How and when books will be delivered
Note: Our website already has most of these. This is just for PayU's compliance team to verify the store is genuine and active.
8

Wait for Approval & Get API Keys

Day 1 — Today

Complete signup, verify PAN, verify bank account, upload all documents

Day 1–2 — Review

PayU team reviews documents and visits our website. They may call on the registered mobile number if any document needs clarification.

Day 2 — Approval Email

You receive an email confirming account is activated. The dashboard shows Production Keys (Merchant Key + Salt)

Day 2 onwards — Live Payments

Devotees can now pay via UPI, QR Code, Cards, Net Banking on our book store

Test mode is available immediately after signup — our developer can test the payment flow before going live, without waiting for full approval.

Common Questions

QuestionAnswer
Is GST number required?No. Religious trusts are GST exempt. Leave the GSTIN field blank.
Is registration free?Yes. PayU account registration is completely free.
What is the transaction fee?Approximately 2% per transaction for UPI/Cards. No setup charges.
When do we receive money?PayU settles payments to our bank account within 1–2 working days.
What if a document is rejected?PayU will email the reason. Reupload the correct document. Usually resolved in 1 day.
Can devotees pay via UPI / QR?Yes. Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM — all UPI apps work.
Is international payment supported?Not by default for trusts. Only Indian payments are enabled initially.
What if I get stuck during signup?Call PayU support or use live chat on the dashboard.
