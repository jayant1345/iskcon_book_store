4

Documents to Upload

Please keep the following documents ready as scanned copies or clear photographs (JPG/PDF):

A) Trust / Entity Document (any ONE of these)

  • Registered Trust Deed
    Most important document — the original registration document of the trust
  • Registration Certificate
    From Charity Commissioner Office (if separately registered)
  • 12A Certificate
    Income tax exemption certificate — very helpful to show legitimacy

B) Trustee / Authorised Person Identity (BOTH required)

  • PAN Card of Trustee
    The trustee who is signing up must provide their personal PAN card
  • Aadhaar Card of Trustee
    Front and back of Aadhaar card — used for identity verification

C) Bank Account Proof (any ONE of these)

  • Cancelled Cheque
    Must show the trust's name printed on it — most preferred option
  • Bank Statement (last 3 months)
    First page showing account number, IFSC, and trust name
  • Bank Passbook (first page)
    Should clearly show trust name, account number, and IFSC
Important: The bank account must be in the trust's name, not a personal account. Payments from devotees will be settled into this account.

Cashfree will send a Re. 1 (one rupee) test deposit to verify the account is correct. This is automatic and takes a few minutes. No action needed from your side.
6

KYC Verification (Identity Check)

Cashfree will verify the trustee's identity. There are two possible paths:

Path A — Automatic (Best case)

If the trustee's PAN/Aadhaar is already in the government's Central KYC database, Cashfree fetches details automatically.

No video call needed. Verification is instant.

Path B — Video KYC Call

A Cashfree agent will call on video (like a WhatsApp video call) for 2–3 minutes.

They will ask the trustee to:
• Show PAN card to camera
• State your name
• Confirm account details
7

Wait for Approval

Day 1 — Today

Complete signup, upload all documents, finish bank verification

Day 2 — Working Day

Cashfree team reviews documents. You may receive a call or email if anything is missing.

Day 2–3 — Approval

Account activated via email. You can now accept real payments from devotees.

Total time: Approximately 2–3 working days from the day you submit all documents. The signup itself takes about 30–45 minutes.

Common Questions

Question Answer

Is there any registration fee? No. Cashfree registration is completely free.
When will we receive payments? Cashfree settles money to your bank account every 1–2 working days.
What is the transaction fee? Approximately 1.75%–2% per transaction. No hidden charges.
What if a document is rejected? Cashfree will email the reason. Re-upload the correct document. Usually resolved in 1 day.
Can devotees pay by UPI / QR Code? Yes! Once active, devotees can pay via UPI QR, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, cards etc.
