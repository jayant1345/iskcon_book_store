🎙 Review Video Upload Guide

ISKCON Book Store — Admin Instructions

Purpose: This guide explains how to record a devotee review video, upload it to YouTube, and add it to a book page on the ISKCON Book Store website.

What You Need

  • A short 30-second video of a devotee talking about a book
  • A computer or phone with internet
  • YouTube account login details (provided by store owner)
  • Access to the store Admin Panel

Part 1 — Record the Video

1
Ask a devotee to record a short video on their phone The video should be around 30 seconds. They can speak in Gujarati, Hindi, or English.
2
What they should say in the video:
  • Which book they are talking about
  • What the book is about
  • How this book helped them / benefited them
  • Why other devotees should read this book
3
Send the video file Devotee sends the recorded video to admin via WhatsApp or Email.

Part 2 — Upload Video to YouTube

⚠ Important: We upload to YouTube because the store server deletes uploaded files on restart. YouTube stores the video permanently and it plays directly on the book page. Set visibility to Unlisted so it does not appear in YouTube search.
1
Open YouTube Go to youtube.com on your computer → Sign in with the provided YouTube account.
2
Click "+ Create" button It is at the top right corner of the YouTube page. From the dropdown menu that appears, click "Upload video".
3
Select the video file Click "Select files" → find the video file on your computer → click Open. The video will start uploading. Wait for the progress bar to complete.
4
Fill in the title Type a simple title, for example:
Bhagavad Gita Review - Devotee Experience
Description can be left empty.
5
Click Next → Next → Next Skip through all the extra screens without changing anything. Just keep clicking the Next button (3 times total).
6
Set visibility to UNLISTED ← Most Important Step On the last screen "Save or publish" you will see three options. Select Unlisted:
◯ Private Only you can see ● Unlisted ← SELECT THIS Anyone with the link can see ◯ Public Everyone can see
7
Click Save YouTube will save the video and show you the video page.
8
Copy the video link The link appears at the bottom of the screen. It looks like this:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ABC123xyz
Copy this link. You will need it in the next step.

Part 3 — Add Video to Book in Admin Panel

1
Open the Admin Panel Go to your store website and add /admin/login at the end of the web address.
Enter your admin username and password.
2
Go to Books Click "Books" in the left side menu.
3
Find the book and click Edit Find the book you want to add the video to. Click the pencil (Edit) icon next to that book.
4
Scroll to the bottom of the page You will see a section called "Reader Review / Book Testimonial".
5
Paste the YouTube link In the green box labelled "YouTube / Vimeo Link" — paste the YouTube link you copied in Part 2, Step 8.
6
Add testimonial text (optional but recommended) In the text box on the left side, type what the devotee said about the book. This text appears next to the video on the book page.
7
Click "Update Book" Click the yellow Update Book button at the bottom of the page.

Part 4 — Verify on Book Page

1
Open the store website Go to your store and find the book you just edited.
2
Scroll down on the book page You will see a section called "What Devotees Say". The YouTube video player will appear there.
3
Click Play to confirm it works The video should play directly on the book page. Customers do not need to go to YouTube.

Quick Reference Summary

Step What to Do Where
1 Get 30-second video from devotee WhatsApp / Email
2 Upload to YouTube youtube.com → + Create → Upload video
3 Set visibility to Unlisted Last screen before saving on YouTube
4 Copy the YouTube video link YouTube video page after saving
5 Admin → Books → Edit book Store Admin Panel
6 Paste link in green YouTube box → Save Bottom of Edit Book page
7 Open book page and verify video plays Store website

Important Notes

  • Always set YouTube visibility to Unlisted — video will not appear in YouTube search but will work on the book page.
  • One video per book — adding a new video replaces the old one.
  • The video plays directly on the book page — customers do not need to visit YouTube.
  • To remove a video — edit the book → clear the YouTube link field → click Update Book.
  • Do not upload large video files using the "Upload Video File" option on the edit page — those files are deleted when the server restarts.
